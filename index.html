AI Assessment

Point your camera.
Get instant risk scores.

Capture a worker performing a task using your phone camera or upload from your gallery. Ergzo's AI analyses body posture, maps joint angles, and scores the task using your chosen assessment method — REBA, RULA, ROSA, WISHA, SNOOK, NIOSH, WERA, QEC, CMDQ, JSI, MAC, OCRA, MANTRA, OWAS or PATH. Results in under 5 minutes, no sensors needed.

📷 Phone Camera 🖼️ Gallery Upload 🤖 AI Posture Detection 🦴 Body Part Risk Map 🎯 Instant Score
Intervention Tracking

Measure what actually improves.

Re-assess after implementing a fix — a new chair, a workstation adjustment, a task redesign. Ergzo automatically compares pre and post-intervention sessions side by side, showing exact score changes, body part improvements, and estimated injury risk reduction. Prove ROI to management with data, not guesswork.

🔁 Before & After 📉 Risk Reduction 💰 Injury Cost Saved 📅 Assessment Trends
Instant Reports

Professional reports.
One tap.

Every session auto-generates a complete ergonomic assessment report — risk scores, body part map, root cause, recommendations, and a corrective action plan. Export as a branded PDF to share with clients or management. Download raw data in CSV or Excel for compliance filing.
