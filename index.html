AI Assessment

Point your camera.
Get instant risk scores.

Capture a worker performing a task using your phone camera or upload from your gallery. Ergzo's AI analyses body posture, maps joint angles, and scores the task using your chosen assessment method — in under 5 minutes, no sensors or wearables needed.

📷 Phone Camera 🖼️ Gallery Upload 🤖 AI Posture Analysis 🦴 Body Part Risk Map 🎯 Instant Risk Score
Intervention Tracking

Measure what actually improves.

Re-assess after implementing a fix — a new chair, a workstation change, a task redesign. Ergzo automatically compares pre and post-intervention sessions, showing exact score changes, body part improvements, and estimated injury risk reduction.

🔁 Before & After 📉 Risk Reduction 💰 Injury Cost Saved 📅 Assessment Trends
Instant Reports

Professional reports.
One tap.

Every session auto-generates a complete ergonomic assessment report — risk scores, body part map, root cause, recommendations, and a corrective action plan. Export as PDF, CSV, or Excel instantly.
