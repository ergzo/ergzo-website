AI Assessment

Point your camera.
Get instant risk scores.

Capture a worker performing a task using your phone camera or upload from your gallery. Ergzo’s AI analyses body posture, maps joint angles, and scores the task using REBA, RULA, ROSA, WISHA, SNOOK, NIOSH, WERA, QEC, CMDQ, JSI, MAC, OCRA, MANTRA, OWAS or PATH. ToolFull nameBest forBody region
