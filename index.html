Features

Everything ergonomics. In one app.

Smart, flexible, and built around the way real safety professionals work.

15 Validated Assessment Tools

REBA, RULA, ROSA, WISHA, SNOOK, NIOSH, WERA, QEC, CMDQ, JSI, MAC, OCRA, MANTRA, OWAS & PATH — all built in, all science-backed.

Camera & Gallery Analysis

Assess live from your phone camera or upload from gallery. No wearables, no sensors, no special hardware required.

Body Part Risk Map

Colour-coded diagram shows exactly which joints and body segments are at highest risk, with joint-level breakdown.

Before & After Comparison

Record pre and post-intervention sessions. Ergzo automatically calculates and displays the exact improvement in risk scores.

Team Analytics Dashboard

Department-wise risks, assessment trends, improvement rates, risk distribution charts, and estimated injury cost per worker.

Multi-format Export

Download every session as a professional PDF report or raw data in CSV/Excel — instantly, with one tap.
