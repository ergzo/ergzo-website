Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026  ·  Ergzo Solutions Private Limited

Ergzo Solutions Private Limited (“Ergzo”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, retain, and protect personal data when you use the Ergzo mobile application, the Ergzo website, and related services used for ergonomic screening, assessment workflows, reporting, follow-up tracking, and workplace analytics.

1. Scope and Roles

Ergzo is primarily offered for workplace use by enterprise and institutional customers, including environment, health and safety teams, medical officers, HR teams, plant managers, factory managers, ergonomists, assessors, and authorized viewers. Workers do not currently maintain direct end-user accounts in the app, although assessment records may relate to a worker or a pseudonymous worker profile created by an authorized company user.

Depending on the context, Ergzo may act as an independent data fiduciary/controller for website, support, account administration, and product operations, and may also act as a processor/service provider on behalf of a customer organization for workplace assessment records handled within the service.

ℹ️

Workplace use: Ergzo is designed for adults in workplace settings and is not intended for children or consumer use.

2. Data We Collect

Account and organization data

For enterprise access and account administration, we may process user and organization information such as name, business email address, company name, designation, access role, team membership, company identifier, and authentication records.

Assessment and ergonomic data

When an authorized user performs an assessment, Ergzo may process posture keypoints, joint angles, ergonomic scores, risk levels, timestamps, department information, task or scene context, joint risk observations, intervention-related status, and associated analytical outputs. These data points may be linked to a worker name or a pseudonymous/default worker label such as “Person #” depending on customer workflow choices.

Health-related self-reported data

Where a customer organization uses the Cornell Musculoskeletal Discomfort Questionnaire (CMDQ) module, Ergzo processes self-reported discomfort information: the body region affected, how often discomfort occurred, how severe it was, and whether it interfered with normal work activities. These responses are linked to a worker or a pseudonymous worker label and are stored in Ergzo’s cloud environment in India alongside the related assessment record. Ergzo treats this as health-related information and applies the access restrictions described in Section 9.

Media and local device data

Photos, imported gallery media, locally recorded assessment videos, and optional screen-recorded assessment replays may be created or processed on the device for ergonomic analysis and review. Raw assessment media remains on the user’s device and is not uploaded to Ergzo’s servers. Where a user saves or exports media — including saving an assessment recording to the device photo library — that file passes outside the app, as described in Section 4.

Device and messaging identifiers

To deliver operational push notifications, Ergzo processes a device messaging token issued by the platform’s notification service, together with the authenticated user identifier assigned to your account. These identifiers are used to route notifications and to associate records with the correct account. They are not used for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

Company assets and operational content

The app may process company logos, worker photos, department names, team structures, session names, notes, and other organization-provided content needed to support assessment workflows and reporting.

Website and support data

When you visit the website, contact us, request a demo, or communicate with support, we may collect standard website, correspondence, and business-contact information needed to respond, provide access, maintain security, and administer our services.

3. How We Use Personal Data

  • To authenticate authorized users and administer enterprise access.
  • To conduct ergonomic screening and produce scores, risk indicators, recommendations, reports, summaries, and dashboard views.
  • To record and analyse self-reported musculoskeletal discomfort where a customer organization uses the CMDQ module, and to relate it to assessed ergonomic risk.
  • To support before-and-after intervention analysis, follow-up workflows, trend analysis, and safety improvement tracking.
  • To allow locally saved review of assessment media and optional on-device screen-recorded assessment overlays.
  • To send operational notifications such as follow-up reminders and weekly reports.
  • To maintain product security, prevent misuse, troubleshoot incidents, and improve product performance.
  • To comply with legal obligations, contractual obligations, safety governance requirements, and legitimate business recordkeeping needs.
✅

No sale of personal data: Ergzo does not sell personal data and is not designed for advertising profiling or cross-app tracking.

4. Permissions and Device Access

Ergzo requests or may request only the permissions reasonably necessary for assessment features, optional local media handling, and related workflows.

Camera

The camera is used to capture posture or movement needed for ergonomic screening and assessment. Camera access is used only within relevant workflows initiated by the user. Pose analysis is performed on the device; camera video is not transmitted to Ergzo’s servers.

Photos / Gallery

Photo library or media access may be used to import existing photos or videos for local ergonomic analysis and to add optional company logos or related assets. Imported media is processed on the device.

Saving to your photo library

Where an assessment recording is saved to your device’s photo library, that file becomes part of your device’s photo storage and may be synchronised to a personal cloud photo service such as iCloud Photos or Google Photos, according to your own device settings. Ergzo has no access to, and no control over, files once they are in your photo library. We recommend saving assessment recordings to your photo library only where your organization’s policy permits it.

Screen Recording

Optional screen recording may be used to create a local replay of the assessment with posture analysis, score overlays, and face blur for later review by the authorized user. This feature is optional and is not designed to capture content from other apps.

Calendar

Where you choose to add a reassessment date to your device calendar, Ergzo writes that event so you receive a reminder when a follow-up ergonomic assessment is due. Ergzo does not read your existing calendar entries.

Microphone

Ergzo does not record or process audio. The app declares no microphone permission on iOS or Android, and assessment screen recordings are silent.

Notifications

Notification permissions are used for operational reminders such as high-risk follow-up prompts, task assignment alerts, and weekly reporting notifications. Ergzo does not use push notifications for unrelated promotional marketing.

⚠️

Permission note: Some features may be unavailable if a required permission is denied.

5. AI-Generated Recommendations and Action Plans

Ergzo offers an optional AI feature that generates recommendations or action plans when a user affirmatively invokes the relevant in-app control. This feature is not required to conduct an assessment or to view baseline scores.

When a user invokes the AI feature, Ergzo transmits a limited, non-media data payload to Google LLC, which operates the Gemini API used to generate recommendations. That payload consists of:

  • REBA or RULA score, joint risk scores, and whether the assessed posture is seated or standing;
  • task type, work environment, and session duration;
  • the worker’s role, work type and height, where those have been recorded;
  • self-reported CMDQ discomfort information — body region, frequency and severity; and
  • titles and status of previous ergonomic interventions recorded for that worker.

Worker names, photographs, video, screen recordings and audio are not transmitted for this feature. Ergzo uses a paid Google Gemini API tier under which Google does not use submitted content to train or improve its models. Google provides protections for this data equivalent to those described in this policy, and processes it only to return the requested output.

The AI feature is never invoked automatically. It runs only when a user affirmatively selects the AI recommendation control for a specific assessment, and no data is transmitted to Google unless and until that control is used. Because the payload includes health-related information, customer organizations should decide as a matter of policy whether their assessors may use this feature.

AI outputs are generated automatically and require human review. Customers and users remain responsible for evaluating whether any recommendation is appropriate for their workplace, workforce, operational constraints, and applicable health and safety obligations.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

Ergzo may disclose personal data or related records only in limited circumstances and only to the extent reasonably necessary for the stated purpose.

  • Within the customer organization: authorized admins, assessors, viewers, managers, and other role-based personnel may access relevant data according to permissions configured for that company.
  • Service providers: Ergzo uses Google Cloud and Firebase services (authentication, database, serverless functions, crash and performance diagnostics, and push notification delivery) to operate the service. Personal data held by Ergzo is stored in the asia-south1 (Mumbai, India) region. These providers are engaged under contract, process data only on Ergzo’s instructions for the purposes described here, and are required to afford protections equivalent to those described in this policy.
  • AI provider: where a user invokes the optional AI feature, the limited non-media payload described in Section 5 is transmitted to Google LLC to generate the requested recommendations or action plans.
  • Customer API access: where a customer organization’s plan includes API access, its administrators may generate API keys that allow that organization’s own systems to retrieve its worker, department, session and assessment records from Ergzo programmatically. Data retrieved this way leaves Ergzo’s environment and is thereafter controlled by that customer organization. Ergzo logs API access for audit purposes.
  • Legal and safety reasons: Ergzo may disclose information if required by law, legal process, binding regulatory request, or where necessary to protect rights, security, safety, or investigate misuse.
  • Corporate transaction: if Ergzo undergoes a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, or sale of assets, data may be transferred subject to appropriate confidentiality and lawful-use safeguards.

Ergzo does not sell personal data to data brokers and does not share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Marketing or Demonstration Footage

Any use of real worker imagery or factory recordings for testimonials, demonstrations, client onboarding materials, case studies, website content, or marketing materials occurs only under a separate and specific consent process appropriate to that purpose. Such use is never assumed from ordinary assessment participation alone.

7. Retention, Deletion, and Record Integrity

Ergzo retains personal data and related records only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including service delivery, enterprise administration, workplace safety recordkeeping, trend analysis, intervention tracking, compliance, dispute handling, security, and legitimate business operations.

Certain assessment and session records may be retained even where a customer can delete or archive other entities such as workers, departments, or related organizational structures. This design preserves audit integrity, reduces the risk of selective underreporting, maintains before-and-after intervention histories, and supports longitudinal ergonomic and safety analysis.

Where a worker identifier is removed from an assessment record, Ergzo may instead display a default or pseudonymous label rather than the original worker name, subject to technical and legal limitations.

Where you submit an erasure request through the app, we suspend the account immediately and confirm in writing within 30 days what has been erased and what has been retained, together with the lawful basis for retaining it.

⚠️

Limitations on deletion: Erasure or deletion rights may be limited where retention is required for legal compliance, contractual obligations, safety record integrity, fraud or misuse prevention, defense of legal claims, or another valid lawful basis recognized by applicable law.

8. Your Rights and Choices

As an India-first service, Ergzo provides notices and controls aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, while also preparing for broader rights frameworks applicable in other regions. Depending on the relationship involved, the applicable law, and whether Ergzo acts as a controller/data fiduciary or processor/service provider, individuals may have rights to:

  • request access to a summary of the personal data being processed and the processing activities undertaken;
  • request correction, completion or updating of inaccurate, misleading or incomplete personal data;
  • request erasure of personal data, subject to the lawful retention exceptions described in Section 7;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, with effect for future processing;
  • seek grievance redressal through the process described in Section 15; and
  • nominate another individual to exercise your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in the event of your death or incapacity. To register a nomination, contact us using the details in Section 15.

The app provides two of these controls directly. Under Legal & Support, Data Export returns a machine-readable copy of the personal data Ergzo holds about your account, and Request account deletion submits a formal erasure request.

Where Ergzo processes data on behalf of a customer organization, requests relating to worker-linked assessment records may need to be routed through the relevant customer organization that controls the underlying workplace assessment program. We will tell you when we do so. Ergzo may also require verification of identity, authority, or organizational role before acting on a request.

9. Security and Safeguards

Ergzo uses reasonable technical, organizational, and administrative safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, misuse, and destruction. These measures include tenant isolation, so that data belonging to one customer organization is accessible only to authenticated, active members of that organization; role-based administrative controls; encryption of the local database on the device; encryption in transit with certificate pinning; app and device attestation checks; cloud-service security controls; pseudonymization measures such as face blur and worker label substitution; audit logging of privileged operations; internal access restrictions; and review of service-provider protections.

No platform, application, network, or storage environment can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure. Users and customer organizations are responsible for protecting device access, passwords, API keys, exported media, and any information they choose to share outside Ergzo.

10. Personal Data Breach

If Ergzo becomes aware of a personal data breach affecting personal data we hold, we will intimate each affected data principal without undue delay, describing the nature and extent of the breach, its likely consequences, the measures we have taken to mitigate it, and the steps the individual may take to protect their interests. We will also report the breach to the Data Protection Board of India, and will provide the Board with a detailed report within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, or such longer period as the Board allows.

Where a breach affects personal data we process on behalf of a customer organization, we will notify that organization without undue delay so that it can meet its own obligations as data fiduciary.

11. Legal Basis and International Users

Ergzo currently positions India as the primary governing privacy regime. If Ergzo expands internationally, additional region-specific disclosures, transfer mechanisms, or legal-basis descriptions may be added for jurisdictions such as the EEA, United Kingdom, California, and other applicable regions.

Where Ergzo processes worker assessment records on behalf of a customer organization, that organization is the data fiduciary and generally relies on the legitimate uses recognized in Section 7(i) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, which permit processing for purposes of employment and for safeguarding the employer from loss or liability. Workers are not asked to consent to workplace safety assessment through the app.

Consent is sought separately, and specifically, for uses outside that employment purpose — in particular the use of the optional AI feature described in Section 5, and any use of worker imagery for marketing, demonstration or case-study purposes described in Section 6.

For its own activities as a data fiduciary — website, support, account administration and product operations — Ergzo may rely on consent, contract performance, legitimate uses recognized by applicable law, legal obligations, or another recognized lawful basis depending on the service context and the data involved.

12. Children and Age Limitations

Ergzo is intended for workplace use involving adults and is not directed to children. Ergzo does not knowingly offer the service as a child-directed product and does not process children’s personal data as part of its product design.

13. Worker Notification in Assessment Workflows

Before an assessment workflow begins, the app presents a session-start notice to the authorized user. That notice records that workers in the session have been informed they are being recorded or assessed for ergonomic risk assessment, that faces are blurred in the recording, that video remains on the device, and that assessment scores and records are stored securely in India and visible to authorized safety staff at that company.

This notice is a record that workers have been informed. It is not a consent mechanism, and it does not replace the customer organization’s own obligations to notify its workforce about workplace safety monitoring, or any obligation it may have under applicable employment or occupational-safety law.

14. Changes to This Policy

Ergzo may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product changes, legal developments, platform requirements, security improvements, or operational changes. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date, and where required, additional notice may be provided in-app, by email, or through another appropriate channel.
